Second estimator · separate engine, separate API

Government Building Cost Prediction — area, storeys, quality, location

A Gram Panchayat office, a school block and an urban health centre are not road corridors, so they are not priced by the road engine and they are not classified by the trained models either — the classifier was fitted on sanctioned scheme projects, not on a 1,200 sq ft panchayat building. This page answers the question that model was never trained for, with one published rate per square foot and every factor it is multiplied by printed on screen.

Mini project by Vishal Raghunath Chatur and Mohsin Jabiulla Balgyar, under the guidance of Tapase Miss — B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science Engineering, DBATU. Same team, same repository, one extra estimator.

    What this number is

    • A planning-grade rate estimate: one standard structural frame at a published ₹ per square foot, times five stated factors.
    • Reproducible — the same inputs give the same rupees, with no random component and no model call.
    • Checkable by hand: the basis line prints the full multiplication, and the work split adds back to its result to the rupee.
    • Accompanied by a site duration from a sq-ft-per-day crew productivity rate plus fixed mobilisation days.

    What it is not

    • Not a trained-model output. Nothing on this page reads the KNN or Random Forest classifier, so no measured metric applies to it.
    • No land, no boundary wall, no furniture, no specialist medical equipment — the three biggest surprises on a real panchayat or health-centre bill.
    • No state SGDA rate schedule, no GST or labour-ceiling variation, no tender escalation, no monsoon idle days.
    • Not a structural design: a hospital wing at 3 storeys may need a frame this rate does not describe at all.
    • The site names come from the same generated hierarchy the route planner uses — real districts and coordinates, placeholder taluka and village names.
